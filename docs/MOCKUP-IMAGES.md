# Adult-store mockup imagery

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool for the Vibe-ration design. Subjects are unbranded retail sex toys and care accessories, shown as product photography with no people or sexual scenes.

The complete prompt set, asset filenames and dimensions are recorded in `tools/mockup-images.json`. The final assets are JPEG encodings of the generated originals; compositions were not retouched or substituted with vector illustrations.

Product photos have a 4:5 composition; category photos are landscape. Matching images appear in the local collection, search results, product gallery, recommendations and sample cart. Every sample product has a corresponding detail preview.

Actual image-picker selections take priority over theme mockups. The global `vr_mockup_images` setting can disable section and empty-catalogue fallbacks. When this is disabled, neutral Photo placeholders return wherever no actual image has been selected.

These visuals do not establish actual stock, specifications, prices or product claims. Replace them with confirmed catalogue photography for the connected store. No Admin products or inventory were created as part of the mockup work.
